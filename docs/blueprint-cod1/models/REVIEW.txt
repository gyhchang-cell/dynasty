Textures: unmodified existing Dynasty pixel atlases, mapped through continuous opaque
2x2, 4x4 and 8x8 native swatches. UV dimensions follow each face's actual dimensions up to
the selected patch size. Of 2112 cube faces, 1402 use more than one pixel; the remaining
710 are narrow edges, rivets, claws and similarly small surfaces. The preview now displays
the actual atlas texture with nearest-neighbour sampling, not one averaged colour per cube.
Dao + shield use royal_guard.png; priest uses imperial_soldier.png; shanxiao uses nian_beast.png.
The possession-effect icon is an unchanged copy of internal_injury.png at its native 18x18 size.
No raster image was generated, resampled or painted for this pass.

Generator validation: unique bone names, parents exist, positive cube sizes, full human joint
chains, animation bones exist, all numeric channels finite, keyframes within declared durations,
expected attack contact/launch ticks exist, and all face UVs remain within the image and
sample only opaque source texels. Validation passes for all four assets.

Limited refinement pass: mask_left and mask_right are independent children of mask.
They remain joined until death tick 12, then separate, turn and land as two pieces.
The living silhouette, principal rig proportions, attack timings and all combat code are unchanged.
The shanxiao retains the original cod1 blue/green face and ordinary fangs; no red-face/gold-fang
design change was made. No climb-specific root pitch was added in this resource pass.

Death-completion pass (resources only, awaiting the separate actual-client death QA):
- Dao: the right shoulder, elbow and wrist drive a downward planting motion. After tick 18
  the independent dao bone compensates for every parent transform, keeping its world blade
  vertical and its point 0.18 model units into the floor while the body falls to the left.
  The helmet drops and rolls twice, ending on the floor. These are authored bones, not physics.
- Priest: 26 identity body_* child bones contain the same original anatomy surface cubes.
  Their scale reaches 0.001 by tick 24; joint parents and original robe/sleeve pieces remain.
  The robe, sleeves, necklace, staff and crown independently collapse onto the ground.
  The two mask halves lie apart in front. No cube or atlas pixel was added or removed.
- Shanxiao: waist, neck, head, both shoulders/elbows/wrists, thighs/knees/ankles and tail
  articulate into a curled side-lying body; this no longer consists of just root/chest rotation.
  Root height follows the lowest posed vertex for floor contact. Fur fading is renderer-owned.
All 26 non-death clips are exactly equal to the prior frozen baseline. Folding body_* cubes
back into their identical parents reproduces the entire prior living geometry byte-for-byte
as parsed JSON. The three other geometries and shield death clip are unchanged.

BLUEPRINT_BROWSER_PATH; defaults point to this machine's already installed local dependencies.
There are no external network downloads.

LIMITS: These are local resource previews, not Minecraft runtime screenshots or multiplayer tests.
No in-game verification is claimed here. In-game locomotion foot slide, wall contacts, held gear
under all attack transitions, resource reloads and observer synchronization still require runtime QA.
Atlas reuse now provides real block-like native colour variation. Its material palette still
inherits the existing artwork: some narrow bronze trim reads copper-brown and some small
faces remain uniform. This is not bespoke painted texture art. The empty robe and mask are
authored visual-only bones, not physically simulated cloth or colliding fragment entities.
Full locomotion clearance, wall-facing climb pose, inertial cloth/streamer motion and
particle/fragment timing still require separate actual-client review or further implementation.
