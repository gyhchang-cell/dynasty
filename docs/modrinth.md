# Dynasty: Empire of the Dragon Throne

A **complete** Eastern Dynasty mod for Minecraft **1.20.1 (Forge 47.x)** — not a proof of concept.

## ✦ Two real-terrain dimensions
- **Celestial Dynasty (天朝·龙庭)** — vanilla-grade noise terrain (continents, mountains, canyons,
  aquifers). Marble bedrock, sea level 63, bright skies yet monsters roam in daylight.
- **Underworld (地府)** — blackstone depths with a lava sea below y=32, closed ceiling, eternal night.
- **6 custom biomes**: Celestial Plains / Jade Forest / Dragon Ridge / Celestial Sea /
  Underworld Wastes / Soul River.
- Jade and Dragon Crystal ores generate in both dimensions.

## ✦ Grand structures (real worldgen, `/locate`-able)
- **Imperial Palace** (48×48): three-tier terrace, crenellated walls, four corner towers,
  Hall of Supreme Harmony (double-eave roof, golden columns, dragon throne, plaque),
  imperial garden, colonnade lanterns and treasure chests.
- **Imperial Mausoleum**: corridor, twin side chambers, burial chamber, sarcophagus and grave goods.
- `/locate structure examplemod:palace` · `/locate structure examplemod:imperial_tomb`

## ✦ Keju exam GUI + dynasty stats
- Right-click the **Exam Paper** item (or `/dynasty keju`) to open a quiz screen with three options.
- 12 official ranks: Commoner → Student → … → Chancellor → Prince Regent.
- Merit / Loyalty / Rebellion stats persisted per player; rebellion at 100 % triggers a rebel uprising.

## ✦ Army formations
`/dynasty army formation <square|wedge|crane|line|circle> [1-64]` — imperial guards follow you,
fight for you and teleport back when left behind.

## ✦ Mobs & bosses
- Minister (chat + gifts), Assassin (stealth & poison), Archer (bow volleys), Royal Guard (heavy armour),
  Rebel Soldier, Nian Beast (fears firecrackers).
- Mythical beasts: Qilin, Phoenix, Nine-tailed Fox — each with unique drops.
- Bosses: **Dragon Emperor** (400 HP, dragon fireballs, summons guards), **Rebel General**
  (charges, summons rebels), **Eunuch Mastermind** (blindness/weakness, summons assassins)
  and the classic **Undead First Emperor**.

## ✦ Potions, pills & food
- Brewing-stand potions: Dragon's Might / Iron Wall / Swift Wind / Intimidation / Loyalty /
  Mandate of Heaven (with splash & lingering variants).
- Pills and dishes: longevity pill, focus pill, healing salve, mooncake, dumplings, candied
  hawthorn, dragon-beard candy, hotpot, tea, wine, immortal peach.

## ✦ Real-date festivals
Spring Festival (Feb 1–20, fireworks & the Nian Beast), Dragon Boat (Jun 1–10), Mid-Autumn
(Sep 15–25, jade rabbits), Double Ninth (Oct 1–10), New Year's Eve (Dec 31–Jan 2).
Manual trigger: `/dynasty festival <id>`.

## ✦ Built-in Codex
A 12-page in-game guide (right-click the **Dynasty Codex**) — **no Patchouli or other dependency required**.

## Installation
1. Minecraft **1.20.1** with **Forge 47.x**
2. Drop `dynasty-1.0.0.jar` into `.minecraft/mods/`
3. No dependencies.
