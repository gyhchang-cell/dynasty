## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless tileable diffuse/albedo texture for the cloth of an ACTUAL 3D Guan Yu guardian statue inside Minecraft, not a concept illustration. Generate one square edge-to-edge flat textile swatch viewed exactly orthographically overhead. Rich deep emerald / malachite green silk brocade ground, fine antique gold embroidery of traditional Chinese ruyi cloud curls interleaved with restrained scrolling small leaves and subtle scaled wave motifs. Dense, exquisitely fine embroidered stitches and subtle silk weave at close inspection, tasteful ceremonial Chinese temple statue aesthetic. The major motifs must be clear continuous elegant curved strokes, not chaotic flecks. Roughly 75 percent green textile negative space and 25 percent aged gold embroidery. Motifs arranged evenly across the ENTIRE swatch; exact horizontal and vertical seamless tiling so boundaries meet, no frame, no border, no center medallion. Use restrained neutral flat diffuse illumination, no cast shadows, no directional highlight, no lighting gradient, no baked folds or 3D perspective. No face, no figure, no dragon head, no weapons, no written symbols or text, no logo, no watermark. Production-ready bitmap surface texture, detailed high-resolution square.

The generated opposite edges are not assumed mathematically seamless. The shader uses mirrored UV sampling to keep joins continuous. No user reference photograph is used as a billboard or embedded in the mod.
