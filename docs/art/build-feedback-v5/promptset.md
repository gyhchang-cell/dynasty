# Armor inventory remaster

Mode: built-in image_gen. Cropping and resizing only; original images retained in sources/.

## general

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: Minecraft Dynasty mod inventory armor icon sheet, one coordinated general armor set. A strict 2x2 equal square grid on genuine transparent alpha background, NO drawn grid lines, NO text, no character. Top left helmet, top right chestplate, bottom left leggings, bottom right pair of boots. Each complete item centered in its own quarter with 15% clear padding and no overlap. Recognizable Minecraft armor silhouettes: helmet is squared cap with face opening, chestplate squared shoulders short sleeves and rectangular torso, leggings rectangular hip with TWO separated legs, boots TWO squared boots. Rich crafted high-resolution game sprite detailing, crisp hard edge shading, readable at small inventory size, not photorealistic. dark steel lamellar armor, crimson cloth, bronze-gold rivets. Chinese dynasty visual identity but Minecraft blocky wearable equipment shape, no flowing robe silhouette, no icon frames, no background, no drop shadows outside icons.
