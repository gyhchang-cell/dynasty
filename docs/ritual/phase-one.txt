PHASE 1 implementation / 2026-09-30

Scope: existing Forge 1.20.1 / 47.4.10 / Java 17. Reuses Zhenyuan node,
SavedData, renderer, registry and encounter entry. No PHASE 2 intro, new Boss
model, skills or dragon transformation. Existing fights/rewards remain working.

Server timeline: Qinglong 100 ticks; Baihu 64; Zhuque 110; Xuanwu 160.
Third completion: 10 ticks silence, pulse/quake, third flow delayed to tick 32.
Fourth: 20 ticks silence; restart in completion order at 20/26/32/38;
convergence/title at 42; FINAL_OFFERING_READY at 90.
Final: 240 ticks. Four flows enter at 10/20/30/40, stronger absorption at 60,
expansion through 120, compression to 20% at 140, suction/pause through 170,
shockwave/pull/lift at 170, fade at 215..235, existing dimension entry at 240.
Server reserves each tribute immediately, commits activation only after animation.
The stored mask, pending mask, per-pillar clocks, stage clock, completion order,
ready/running/fight/defeated flags survive normal world/server saves and reloads.
Legacy unstarted altars migrate without resetting paid offerings. Existing active
or paused encounters retain their original recovery and single-reward workflow.
Multiple players can supply different pillars. The final payer is the existing
single-encounter participant; bystanders see the ceremony and nearby third omen.
Missing owner or unloaded nodes pause the authoritative clock; no forced loads.
Returning within 64 blocks resumes the paid final ritual without re-payment.

Visuals are bounded client-side geometry, not collectible item entities.
Snapshots use existing BE update packets every four active ticks. Tick-side focus
registration keeps cinema working while looking away. Audio is a transient master
listener gain (no saved option writes), restored when the ritual/focus ends.
Camera/FOV/input effects reset automatically on death, disconnect or dimension change.
Pulses stay below 3 Hz; no rapid full-screen strobing. Normal player movement is
restrained only during the final 70 ticks, without permanent ability flags.
