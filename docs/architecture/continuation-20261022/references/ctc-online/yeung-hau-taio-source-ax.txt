				76 text Hau Wong - Marquis Prince (侯王)
				77 text Hau Wong refers to Yeung Leung-jit(楊亮節), a loyal follower and a marquis of the last Emperor of the Southern Song Dynasty who fled with the royal family to Kowloon driven by the Mongols. Others believe that the temple was built in memory of a villager surnamed Yeung who had cured the Emperor of the Southern Song of an illness when the emperor's family fled to Kowloon City.
